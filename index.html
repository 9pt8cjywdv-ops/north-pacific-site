How the OTC desk works

No exchange screens and no slippage surprises. You agree a price with a trader, and the trade settles straight away from money you've already deposited.

  1. Open your account

    We verify your business, its directors and owners. Our compliance team approves the account, usually within a few working days.

  2. Prefund

    Deposit rand by EFT with your reference, or send USDT to your own Ethereum deposit address.

  3. Ask for a price

    Tell your trader the amount. You get a firm all-in rate, held for long enough to say yes.

  4. Settle and withdraw

    The trade settles at once. Ask us to pay rand to your verified bank account or send USDT to your approved wallet.
